# OPENCLOCK v0
### The Story Behind What Was OPENCLOCK

Four months ago came a moment in my life that I honestly deem crucial to why I decided to share my work with the world. It was the month of April; by this point my LinkedIn was steadily growing, however not at an insane speed. I had not even reached 100 connections yet, and I was still in my junior year of high school. I had been working on my biggest project yet at the time; it was supposed to be an open-source project. 



It was going to be a smart home controller, or “smart clock," as I called it, and I was making it with maximum compatibility in mind. At first, I wanted to make the software an Android ROM rather than a launcher, but due to time constraints and other things that had their issues, I shifted to making a launcher; however, even then I was not able to finish fully. 



The only part that works is the time. Nonetheless, I had to present it at my school’s STREAM Faire, and I was honestly surprised. I presented my design documents, the unfinished launcher, the various phones I used, and the device housing (it got a little bit messed up from sanding; I wish my hand was steadier than it is 😅). 



While assembly may have failed, the project still was received very well. I remember a biomedical engineer at the event asked me where I was planning to go with this project. I mentioned that I wanted to keep working on it, and if I’m being honest, I really do want to revive OPENCLOCK someday, but I decided to post what I had on GitHub so I could look back and see my progress. Maybe someday I will try my hand at developing an Android ROM again.
